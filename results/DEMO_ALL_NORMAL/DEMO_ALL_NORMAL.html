9. Limitations

  • Only 4 variant alleles are recognized (*2, *3A, *3B, *3C), covering ~95% of known TPMT no-function alleles in the population literature; rarer alleles (*4, *5, *6, *8, and others) are not recognized and fall through to an unsupported-allele result rather than being silently mis-called.
  • Simultaneous variants at both the *2 locus and the *3B/*3C loci at once are out of scope; reported as an unsupported allele pattern rather than attempting three-way phasing.
  • A confident Normal Metabolizer (*1/*1) call requires all three defining positions (*2, *3B, *3C) confirmed reference; an unconfirmed *2 locus blocks that specific conclusion even when the *3-family pair looks clean.
  • VCF phase information (| separators, phase blocks) is read but not used by this module's phasing logic.
  • Multi-allelic ALT fields use only the first listed allele; not exercised against a genuinely multi-allelic real case yet.
